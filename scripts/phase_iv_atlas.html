Inspect any tic-tac-toe decision

The original player in repeat 1 always chooses a move preserving its best guaranteed outcome, using one fixed ordering to break ties. Cells are numbered 0–8 across rows. A dot is empty. Search any of the 4,520 reachable decision boards, or follow a move to its resulting board.




V scores the resulting board. Q scores the board and move. Direct selection takes the highest fitted frequency. Von Neumann–Morgenstern (VNM) selection scores the final move of the complete game after both seats follow the fitted direct rule, signed for the player to move. The gap is the highest score minus the next highest. Spread is one sample standard deviation over four fits of this source, two fits on each of two partitions.
